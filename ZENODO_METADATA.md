## Description

Code, frozen outputs, numerical audits, and manuscript materials for *Contagion or Common Factor? Instrumental Identification and Robust Inference in High-Frequency Spillover Networks*. The package develops external-proxy instrumental identification and weak-instrument-robust Anderson–Rubin inference for high-frequency spillover networks with latent common drivers. It includes confirmatory equal-weight and training-frozen PC1 applications, Hansen overidentification diagnostics, corrected temporal placebos, structural-compatibility diagnostics, Monte Carlo experiments, and certified spectral-radius projection audits. Raw Binance aggregate trades are publicly available and are not redistributed; frozen derived outputs used by the manuscript are included.
